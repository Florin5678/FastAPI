# Journal prompts

100 self-reflection prompts for the Journal widget, drawn from psychology
(CBT, DBT, ACT, positive psychology, self-compassion research) and philosophy
(Stoicism, existentialism, contemplative traditions).

**How the widget uses this file**
- One prompt per numbered line: `N. Prompt text — *idea behind it*`.
  The number is the prompt's permanent id (saved with each journal entry), so
  add new prompts at the end instead of renumbering.
- Each day shows one prompt (same prompt all day), in small italic text above
  the writing area, with an option to get a different one.
- The `## Section` a prompt sits in is shown as its source, e.g.
  *Stoicism · dichotomy of control*.

**A note on use.** Journaling supports reflection; it isn't therapy. If a
prompt stirs up something heavy, it's fine to skip it. In a crisis, call
**112** (EU emergency), **Livslinien 70 201 201** (Denmark) or
**0800 801 200** (Romania, Telefonul Antisuicid).

## Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)

1. What thought has taken up the most space in your mind today? Write it down word for word. — *automatic thoughts*
2. Pick one worry from today. What evidence supports it, and what evidence doesn't? — *evidence for and against*
3. If a close friend told you the thought that's bothering you, what would you say to them? — *the double standard*
4. When did you last assume you knew what someone else was thinking? What else might they have been thinking? — *mind reading*
5. Where did you think in all-or-nothing terms today? What does the middle ground look like? — *black-and-white thinking*
6. For something you're worried about: what's the worst that could realistically happen, the best, and the most likely? — *decatastrophizing*
7. Which "should" or "must" did you tell yourself today? How would it sound as a preference instead? — *should statements*
8. Describe a moment today when your mood shifted. What happened just before, and what went through your mind? — *the ABC model*
9. What belief about yourself did you pick up early in life? Is it still accurate today? — *core beliefs*
10. What went well today that you were quick to write off as luck or "not counting"? — *discounting the positive*
11. Is there a feeling you're treating as a fact right now? What would change if it were only a feeling? — *emotional reasoning*
12. What did you avoid today, and what did avoiding it cost you or protect you from? — *avoidance*
13. How strongly do you believe your most stressful thought, from 0 to 100%? Write about it, then rate it again. What changed? — *belief ratings*
14. What label did you put on yourself or someone else today? What would a fairer, more specific description be? — *labeling*
15. What small experiment could you run this week to test a belief that holds you back? — *behavioral experiments*

## Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT)

16. Name the emotions you felt today as precisely as you can. Where did you notice each one in your body? — *naming emotions*
17. What would your wise mind say about a decision you're facing, weighing both what you feel and what you know? — *wise mind*
18. What are you resisting that you can't change right now? What would accepting it, without approving of it, look like? — *radical acceptance*
19. When did you last feel overwhelmed? What helped you come down (breathing, cold water, a walk), or what could have? — *distress tolerance*
20. Where can two things be true at once for you today, like "I'm doing my best, and I can do better"? — *dialectics*
21. What urge did you have today that you didn't act on? What helped you ride it out? — *urge surfing*
22. Did one of today's emotions fit the facts of the situation? If not, what would acting opposite to it look like? — *check the facts, opposite action*
23. What did you do today to protect your mood: sleep, food, movement, rest? What's missing? — *reducing vulnerability*
24. What pleasant thing could you add to your week simply because it feels good? — *building positive experiences*
25. Is there a request, or a "no", you've been putting off? How could you say it clearly and kindly? — *asking assertively*
26. In a recent conversation, how did you balance getting what you wanted, keeping the relationship and keeping your self-respect? — *interpersonal effectiveness*
27. Describe one ordinary moment from today in full detail, without judging it as good or bad. — *observe and describe*

## Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT)

28. What are the three values that matter most to you right now? When did you act on one of them this week? — *values*
29. Which thought keeps hooking you? Write it as "I'm having the thought that…". Does it grip you less? — *cognitive defusion*
30. What would you do tomorrow if your doubts came along for the ride but didn't get to steer? — *willingness*
31. What small, concrete step toward something you care about can you take in the next 24 hours? — *committed action*
32. What story about yourself do you tell most often? Who would you be without it? — *self-as-context*
33. What are you struggling against inside yourself? What has the struggle cost you so far? — *creative hopelessness*
34. What did you notice today that you would normally have rushed past? — *present-moment awareness*
35. If your life were a book, what would you want this chapter to be about? — *values clarification*
36. What uncomfortable feeling could you make room for today instead of pushing it away? — *acceptance*
37. Where did your actions today match the person you want to be, and where didn't they? — *values-consistent action*

## Positive psychology and the science of wellbeing

38. Write down three good things from today and why each one happened. — *three good things*
39. Which of your strengths did you use today? How could you use it in a new way tomorrow? — *character strengths*
40. Who are you grateful for this week? What exactly did they do, and have you told them? — *gratitude*
41. Which moment from today would you like to live again? Replay it slowly, in detail. — *savoring*
42. When did you last lose track of time doing something? What made that possible? — *flow*
43. Imagine your life five years from now, going as well as it realistically could. What does an ordinary day look like? — *best possible self*
44. What gave you energy today, and what drained it? — *energy audit*
45. What act of kindness did you do or receive recently? How did it feel? — *prosocial behavior*
46. What are you looking forward to this week, this month and this year? — *anticipation and hope*
47. Which relationship gave you a sense of belonging lately? What made it feel that way? — *relatedness*
48. Where do you feel yourself growing more capable right now? What skill do you want to build next? — *competence*
49. When did you last feel free to choose how to spend your time? What did you choose? — *autonomy*

## Self-compassion

50. What would you say to yourself right now if you spoke the way a kind, honest coach would? — *self-kindness*
51. What mistake have you been replaying? What would it mean to forgive yourself for it? — *self-forgiveness*
52. Which part of what you're going through is something many people go through too? — *common humanity*
53. What does your inner critic say most often? What is it clumsily trying to protect you from? — *the inner critic*
54. What do you need right now: rest, connection, movement, reassurance, something else? — *meeting your needs*
55. Write a short letter to yourself from someone who loves you unconditionally. — *compassionate letter*

## Stoicism

56. What is within your control today, and what isn't? Where did you spend your energy? — *dichotomy of control*
57. What upset you today: the event itself, or your judgment about it? — *Epictetus on judgments*
58. Imagine losing something you take for granted. How does that change the way you see it tonight? — *negative visualization*
59. What obstacle are you facing? How could it become a chance to practice patience, courage or skill? — *the obstacle is the way*
60. Where did you act with wisdom, courage, justice or self-control today, and where did you fall short? — *the four virtues*
61. If today were your last day, what would you regret not having done or said? — *memento mori*
62. What did you do today out of habit or to win approval, rather than because it was right? — *Seneca's evening review*
63. Which comfort do you depend on most? Could you go without it for a day, just to know you can? — *voluntary discomfort*
64. How does today's frustration look when you picture it from high above, among everyone else's lives? — *the view from above*
65. Who irritated you recently? What might they be struggling with that you can't see? — *Marcus Aurelius on others*
66. What would you do differently if nobody would ever know about it or praise you for it? — *virtue for its own sake*
67. What might go wrong tomorrow, and how would you like to respond if it does? — *premeditation of adversity*

## Existentialism

68. What choices are you making by not choosing? — *Sartre on responsibility*
69. Where are you living by other people's expectations rather than your own? — *authenticity*
70. What gives your life meaning right now, even in small ways? — *making meaning*
71. How do you respond to the fact that your time is limited? Does it frighten you, motivate you, or both? — *finitude*
72. What would you do if you accepted that nobody is coming to give you permission? — *freedom*
73. Describe a time you felt deeply alone. What did it teach you about what you need from others? — *existential isolation*
74. Where are you pushing the same boulder up the hill again? Can you find meaning in the pushing itself? — *Camus and the absurd*
75. Which hardship in your life has changed you for the better? — *Frankl on meaning in suffering*
76. What would one courageous step toward who you could become look like? — *Kierkegaard on becoming*
77. What do you truly want, apart from what you think you're supposed to want? — *bad faith*

## Mindfulness and contemplative traditions

78. Take three slow breaths. What do you notice in your body, in your mind and around you? — *mindful check-in*
79. What did you do on autopilot today? How would it feel to do it tomorrow with full attention? — *beginner's mind*
80. Which feeling came and went today? What does its passing suggest about the feelings you have now? — *impermanence*
81. What are you clinging to: an outcome, a person, an image of yourself? What would loosening your grip feel like? — *non-attachment*
82. Wish yourself, someone you love and someone difficult well. What came up for you? — *loving-kindness*
83. What in your life is already enough? — *contentment*
84. Where did you add suffering today by resisting something painful? — *the second arrow*

## Relationships

85. Who did you feel most yourself with this week? What about them makes that possible? — *secure connection*
86. Is there a conversation you're avoiding? What are you afraid will happen? — *avoiding conflict*
87. When did you last feel misunderstood? What did you wish the other person knew? — *being understood*
88. How did you show care to someone today, and how do you like to receive care? — *giving and receiving*
89. What boundary would make one of your relationships healthier? — *boundaries*
90. Who have you lost touch with that you'd like to hear from? What's stopping you from reaching out? — *reconnection*
91. What pattern keeps repeating in your relationships? Where might it have started? — *attachment patterns*

## Body and behavior

92. How did you sleep, eat and move today, and how did that show up in your mood? — *the mind-body link*
93. What activity reliably lifts your mood a little? When can you fit it into this week? — *behavioral activation*
94. Where are you holding tension in your body right now? What might it be carrying? — *body awareness*
95. What has training or physical effort taught you about discipline or patience lately? — *effort and growth*
96. What habit do you want to build? What's the smallest version of it you could do tomorrow? — *tiny habits*

## Meaning and your future self

97. What would your future self, ten years from now, thank you for doing today? — *future self*
98. What have you learned this month that you want to remember? — *reflection*
99. What are you proud of that nobody else might notice? — *quiet pride*
100. If you could keep only three things from this year, what would they be, and why? — *priorities*
