# Journal prompts

Self-reflection prompts for the Journal widget, inspired by psychology (CBT,
DBT, ACT, positive psychology, self-compassion) and philosophy (Stoicism,
existentialism, contemplative traditions).

**Editing:** one prompt per line as `N. Prompt text`. The number is the
prompt's permanent id (saved with each journal entry), so add new prompts at
the end with the next number instead of renumbering. Any other line (like
this text) is ignored. Push the change and the widget picks it up.

**A note on use.** Journaling supports reflection; it isn't therapy. If a
prompt stirs up something heavy, it's fine to skip it. In a crisis, call
**112** (EU emergency), **Livslinien 70 201 201** (Denmark) or
**0800 801 200** (Romania, Telefonul Antisuicid).

## Prompts

1. What thought has taken up the most space in your mind today? Write it down word for word.
2. Pick one worry from today. What evidence supports it, and what evidence doesn't?
3. If a close friend told you the thought that's bothering you, what would you say to them?
4. When did you last assume you knew what someone else was thinking? What else might they have been thinking?
5. Where did you think in all-or-nothing terms today? What does the middle ground look like?
6. For something you're worried about: what's the worst that could realistically happen, the best, and the most likely?
7. Which "should" or "must" did you tell yourself today? How would it sound as a preference instead?
8. Describe a moment today when your mood shifted. What happened just before, and what went through your mind?
9. What belief about yourself did you pick up early in life? Is it still accurate today?
10. What went well today that you were quick to write off as luck or "not counting"?
11. Is there a feeling you're treating as a fact right now? What would change if it were only a feeling?
12. What did you avoid today, and what did avoiding it cost you or protect you from?
13. How strongly do you believe your most stressful thought, from 0 to 100%? Write about it, then rate it again. What changed?
14. What label did you put on yourself or someone else today? What would a fairer, more specific description be?
15. What small experiment could you run this week to test a belief that holds you back?
16. Name the emotions you felt today as precisely as you can. Where did you notice each one in your body?
17. What would your wise mind say about a decision you're facing, weighing both what you feel and what you know?
18. What are you resisting that you can't change right now? What would accepting it, without approving of it, look like?
19. When did you last feel overwhelmed? What helped you come down (breathing, cold water, a walk), or what could have?
20. Where can two things be true at once for you today, like "I'm doing my best, and I can do better"?
21. What urge did you have today that you didn't act on? What helped you ride it out?
22. Did one of today's emotions fit the facts of the situation? If not, what would acting opposite to it look like?
23. What did you do today to protect your mood: sleep, food, movement, rest? What's missing?
24. What pleasant thing could you add to your week simply because it feels good?
25. Is there a request, or a "no", you've been putting off? How could you say it clearly and kindly?
26. In a recent conversation, how did you balance getting what you wanted, keeping the relationship and keeping your self-respect?
27. Describe one ordinary moment from today in full detail, without judging it as good or bad.
28. What are the three values that matter most to you right now? When did you act on one of them this week?
29. Which thought keeps hooking you? Write it as "I'm having the thought that…". Does it grip you less?
30. What would you do tomorrow if your doubts came along for the ride but didn't get to steer?
31. What small, concrete step toward something you care about can you take in the next 24 hours?
32. What story about yourself do you tell most often? Who would you be without it?
33. What are you struggling against inside yourself? What has the struggle cost you so far?
34. What did you notice today that you would normally have rushed past?
35. If your life were a book, what would you want this chapter to be about?
36. What uncomfortable feeling could you make room for today instead of pushing it away?
37. Where did your actions today match the person you want to be, and where didn't they?
38. Write down three good things from today and why each one happened.
39. Which of your strengths did you use today? How could you use it in a new way tomorrow?
40. Who are you grateful for this week? What exactly did they do, and have you told them?
41. Which moment from today would you like to live again? Replay it slowly, in detail.
42. When did you last lose track of time doing something? What made that possible?
43. Imagine your life five years from now, going as well as it realistically could. What does an ordinary day look like?
44. What gave you energy today, and what drained it?
45. What act of kindness did you do or receive recently? How did it feel?
46. What are you looking forward to this week, this month and this year?
47. Which relationship gave you a sense of belonging lately? What made it feel that way?
48. Where do you feel yourself growing more capable right now? What skill do you want to build next?
49. When did you last feel free to choose how to spend your time? What did you choose?
50. What would you say to yourself right now if you spoke the way a kind, honest coach would?
51. What mistake have you been replaying? What would it mean to forgive yourself for it?
52. Which part of what you're going through is something many people go through too?
53. What does your inner critic say most often? What is it clumsily trying to protect you from?
54. What do you need right now: rest, connection, movement, reassurance, something else?
55. Write a short letter to yourself from someone who loves you unconditionally.
56. What is within your control today, and what isn't? Where did you spend your energy?
57. What upset you today: the event itself, or your judgment about it?
58. Imagine losing something you take for granted. How does that change the way you see it tonight?
59. What obstacle are you facing? How could it become a chance to practice patience, courage or skill?
60. Where did you act with wisdom, courage, justice or self-control today, and where did you fall short?
61. If today were your last day, what would you regret not having done or said?
62. What did you do today out of habit or to win approval, rather than because it was right?
63. Which comfort do you depend on most? Could you go without it for a day, just to know you can?
64. How does today's frustration look when you picture it from high above, among everyone else's lives?
65. Who irritated you recently? What might they be struggling with that you can't see?
66. What would you do differently if nobody would ever know about it or praise you for it?
67. What might go wrong tomorrow, and how would you like to respond if it does?
68. What choices are you making by not choosing?
69. Where are you living by other people's expectations rather than your own?
70. What gives your life meaning right now, even in small ways?
71. How do you respond to the fact that your time is limited? Does it frighten you, motivate you, or both?
72. What would you do if you accepted that nobody is coming to give you permission?
73. Describe a time you felt deeply alone. What did it teach you about what you need from others?
74. Where are you pushing the same boulder up the hill again? Can you find meaning in the pushing itself?
75. Which hardship in your life has changed you for the better?
76. What would one courageous step toward who you could become look like?
77. What do you truly want, apart from what you think you're supposed to want?
78. Take three slow breaths. What do you notice in your body, in your mind and around you?
79. What did you do on autopilot today? How would it feel to do it tomorrow with full attention?
80. Which feeling came and went today? What does its passing suggest about the feelings you have now?
81. What are you clinging to: an outcome, a person, an image of yourself? What would loosening your grip feel like?
82. Wish yourself, someone you love and someone difficult well. What came up for you?
83. What in your life is already enough?
84. Where did you add suffering today by resisting something painful?
85. Who did you feel most yourself with this week? What about them makes that possible?
86. Is there a conversation you're avoiding? What are you afraid will happen?
87. When did you last feel misunderstood? What did you wish the other person knew?
88. How did you show care to someone today, and how do you like to receive care?
89. What boundary would make one of your relationships healthier?
90. Who have you lost touch with that you'd like to hear from? What's stopping you from reaching out?
91. What pattern keeps repeating in your relationships? Where might it have started?
92. How did you sleep, eat and move today, and how did that show up in your mood?
93. What activity reliably lifts your mood a little? When can you fit it into this week?
94. Where are you holding tension in your body right now? What might it be carrying?
95. What has training or physical effort taught you about discipline or patience lately?
96. What habit do you want to build? What's the smallest version of it you could do tomorrow?
97. What would your future self, ten years from now, thank you for doing today?
98. What have you learned this month that you want to remember?
99. What are you proud of that nobody else might notice?
100. If you could keep only three things from this year, what would they be, and why?
